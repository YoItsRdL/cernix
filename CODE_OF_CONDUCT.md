# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Cernix to be free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Things that make this project better:

- Assuming good faith, and giving feedback on the work rather than the person
- Accepting that a maintainer may say no, and that "no" is not hostility
- Being clear about what you actually verified versus what you assumed

Things that aren't acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a [private security advisory](https://github.com/YoItsRdL/cernix/security/advisories/new), which is the private channel this project has. It's not what advisories are designed for, but it's private and it works.

Reports will be reviewed and acted on. The maintainer will respect the reporter's privacy. Responses range from a private warning to a permanent ban, depending on severity.

This is a small project maintained by one person. There's no committee: just an intent to keep it a place people want to contribute to.
