Your photographs stay yours.

Cernix is a program on your computer, not a service you join. Ingesting, culling and editing all work with the internet switched off, and the only thing it ever reaches for is the Drive you point it at.

Ingest

Put the card in. That's the setup.

Cernix sees the card within two seconds and copies everything onto your computer, filed by the day you shot it.

Anything it has already taken is skipped, and nothing on the card is ever changed or deleted.

SD, CFexpress and USB RAW, JPEG, HEIC and video Sorted by the day you shot Never takes the same shot twice Originals untouched
